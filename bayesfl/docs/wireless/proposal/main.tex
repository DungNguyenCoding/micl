\title{Joint Bandwidth and Posterior-Retention Allocation for Digital Wireless FedKLS}
\author{Manuscript formulation and implementation plan}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
This manuscript formulation extends KL-guided selective posterior transmission (FedKLS) to orthogonal digital wireless links with distance-dependent path loss and small-scale fading. Full local Bayesian training and prior-fill posterior aggregation are preserved. A synchronous reporting barrier precedes resource allocation, and a prescribed common round duration determines the available upload interval. Client-specific integer retention counts and bandwidths are jointly selected to minimize weighted local posterior-reconstruction divergence. An exact elimination of bandwidth reduces the problem to a separable integer resource-allocation problem. Lagrangian dual decomposition yields an exact local KL-threshold response, including the bitmap-free dense-message option. The server coordinates these responses through price bisection while maintaining a feasible allocation and a dual lower bound. No predefined list of keep ratios is required. Global optimality is asserted only when supported by a valid objective-gap certificate. An implementation workflow and validation criteria are provided; wireless learning-performance gains are not assumed or reported.
\end{abstract}
